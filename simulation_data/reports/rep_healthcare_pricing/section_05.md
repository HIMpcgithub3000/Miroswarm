## 5. Strategic Policy Directives & Counter-Retaliation Architecture

To prevent compensatory hospital inflation, regulatory bodies must transition from piecemeal line-item caps to:
1. **Standardized Procedure Episode Packages**: Mandatory all-inclusive billing covering room, surgeon, consumables, and OT.
2. **Automated OT Surge Detection**: Flagging hospital bills where OT fee growth exceeds historical standard deviations by >15%.
3. **Real-Time Consumer Dispute Playbooks**: Equipping patients with legally binding protest letters at the point of discharge.
